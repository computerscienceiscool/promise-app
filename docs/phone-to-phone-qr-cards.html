Promise App: Phone A → Relay → Phone B

This is not a direct phone-to-phone connection. Do not scan an old QR window.

Phone A → reachable relay → Phone B

Relay configured in these QRs: __RELAY_URL__

Before starting: Put Phone A and Phone B on genuinely separate networks if proving Internet delivery. Both phones must be able to reach this relay; the phones do not need to reach each other. On each phone, use Check relay and continue only after Relay check: ready.

1. PHONE A: RELAY URL QR

On Phone A tap Scan relay URL QR, then scan this.

This configures Phone A transport only. It is not Phone B’s identity.

2. PHONE B: RELAY URL QR

On Phone B tap Scan relay URL QR, then scan this.

This is the same relay as Phone A. It configures Phone B transport only.

3. PHONE A: PHONE B IDENTITY QR

On Phone A tap Scan recipient identity QR, then scan this.

This selects Phone B as recipient. It is not a relay URL.

Run the proof

  1. Phone B: scan QR 2, tap Check relay, and confirm Relay check: ready.
  2. Phone A: scan QR 1, tap Check relay, and confirm Relay check: ready.
  3. Phone A: scan QR 3. Confirm the recipient field is filled, enter a harmless message, then tap Sign, queue, and upload.
  4. Phone A: record the visible Message CID and wait for Relay accepted <CID>.
  5. Phone B: tap Refresh inbox. Pass only when it reports no failures and its Inbox lists the same CID as VERIFIED.

Meaning: Relay accepted proves the relay stored Phone A’s exact bytes. Phone B’s VERIFIED state proves independent recipient-side verification. Neither means human acceptance of a promise.

Interruption check: disconnect one phone from the relay network, tap Refresh inbox or retry an upload, confirm the actionable failure, reconnect, and repeat the affected action.
